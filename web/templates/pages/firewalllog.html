Settings

Turning this on adds a log statement to every drop rule, so the ruleset is rebuilt when you save. The log statements are rate-limited in the kernel and the table is capped, because a firewall log that fills the disk during an attack is a second outage on top of the first.

Reading the kernel log needs CAP_SYSLOG when kernel.dmesg_restrict=1. If wgrouter cannot read it, the drops still happen and are still counted — only the per-packet detail is missing.
